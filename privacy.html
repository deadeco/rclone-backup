Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026.

Rclone Personal Backup is a personal, non-commercial OAuth application used solely by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive account with Rclone for encrypted GrapheneOS Seedvault backups.

Google user data accessed

With the account owner's explicit OAuth consent, Rclone may list, read, create, modify and delete Google Drive files and folders as permitted by the selected Google Drive OAuth scope. This access is used to store, manage and retrieve encrypted backup files.

Use and storage

Rclone operates locally on the owner's Windows computer. OAuth tokens and configuration are stored locally on that computer; encrypted backup content is held in the owner's Google Drive account. This informational website does not process uploads, collect visitor account data, or operate a server-side database.

Sharing and transfers

The application is for the owner's use only and does not sell Google user data or share it with third-party advertising services. The owner's backup content is transferred between the owner's devices and their own Google Drive account using the Rclone application and related self-managed network tools.

Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive account until the owner deletes them. OAuth authorization can be revoked from Google Account security settings; local Rclone credentials may be deleted from the owner's computer.

Security

Seedvault encrypts backup data before transfer. The owner is responsible for securing devices, local OAuth credentials and backup recovery information.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
